PFOS Fish Guidelines

Many official fish advisories are based on PFOS, one PFAS compound measured in a lab. Here is how one widely used guideline turns a PFOS level into meal advice.

Great Lakes Consortium meal guidelines for PFOS in fish
PFOS in the fish (ppb)Suggested meals
0 to 2.54 or more meals a week
Over 2.5 to 52 meals a week
Over 5 to 101 meal a week
Over 10 to 401 meal a month
Over 40Do not eat

Source: Great Lakes Consortium, Best Practice for PFOS Guidelines, Table 1 (September 2025)

Can I use this with my ForeverCatch estimate?

No. These limits are for lab-measured PFOS. ForeverCatch estimates total PFAS with a model, so its numbers can’t be read against this table.

Advice for your water

States issue fish advisories for specific lakes and rivers. Check your state’s fish advisory program before deciding how often to eat a fish.
